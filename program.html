About Event

This workshop is co-organized by researchers from Mississippi State University, Seoul National University, Inha University, University of Galway, University of Cambridge, Georgia Institute of Technology, Columbia University, North Dakota State University, Stony Brook University, Hong Kong Polytechnic University, and University of Oxford, in collaboration with KSARC.
